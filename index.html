Who knows what, and who's allowed to know it.

Every meeting tells Atlas who was in the room, what was decided and who owns it. Instead of pouring everything into one big pile, Atlas keeps each piece of knowledge attached to the person it came from, with the same access the meeting had.

It follows the room

What was said in a meeting is visible to the people who were there. Everyone else sees that it exists and who to ask.

Private stays private

1:1s and anything you mark private stay with your agent. They never show up in anyone else's answers.

Ask the person, not the pile

When you need context you don't have, Atlas finds who has it and asks their agent for what you're allowed to see.

One agent for every person, organised like your company.

Your agent starts with your meetings. As teammates connect, their agents can ask each other for context, only through what each person allows. Over time every role has an agent, and the whole company has a map of what it knows.
